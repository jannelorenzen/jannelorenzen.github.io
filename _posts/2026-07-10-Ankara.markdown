The first phase of data collection in my new project is complete! A week ago, I returned from Ankara after two intense weeks of collecting production and perception data from Turkish learners of French for our project on the [L3 acquisition of French prosody](https://gepris.dfg.de/gepris/projekt/556832628?language=en). My colleague Özce Özceçelik and I had a great time in Ankara, where we were hosted by the wonderful İpek Pınar Uzun at the NÖROM lab. We are also grateful for our wonderful student assistant Serdar Servet, who helped us immensely with the acquisition and scheduling of participants. Now we are looking forward to presenting first results at the upcoming AMLaP, P&P, and SNL conferences!
